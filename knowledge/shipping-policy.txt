Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental region and 10-14 business days for international orders. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

Orders ship from our fulfillment center within 1-2 business days of being placed, except during major sale events when processing may take up to 4 business days. Customers receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information as soon as the order leaves the warehouse.

If a shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, support should first check the tracking number with the carrier for known service disruptions (weather, customs holds, regional outages) before offering any compensation. If tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days, the package may be considered lost in transit, and a replacement or refund should be offered.

Shipping fees are non-refundable once an order has shipped, except when the delay or loss is the fault of our fulfillment process rather than the carrier.

We are not responsible for delays caused by incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses provided by the customer at checkout; in that case, the customer should be guided to contact the carrier directly or place a new order.
