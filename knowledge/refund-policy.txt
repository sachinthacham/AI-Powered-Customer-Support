Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date, provided the item is unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging. Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable except where required by law.

To request a refund, the customer must contact support with their order number and the reason for the return. Once a return is approved, the customer has 14 days to ship the item back. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

If an order is cancelled before it ships, a full refund is issued automatically within 24 hours - no return shipment is required in that case.

Orders that arrive damaged or defective qualify for a full refund or a free replacement, at the customer's choice, and do not count against the standard 30-day window if reported within 7 days of delivery.

Refunds for orders paid partly with store credit are issued as store credit for that portion, and to the original payment method for the remainder.
